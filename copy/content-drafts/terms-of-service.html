11. Allergic reactions and skin sensitivities

Like any cosmetic or grooming product, Furgenics products may cause allergic reactions, skin irritation, or other adverse reactions in a small number of dogs, even when used as directed. Individual animals may react unpredictably to ingredients that are well-tolerated by most dogs. We make no warranty that our products will be tolerated by every dog.

Before using a Furgenics product on a dog for the first time:

  • Review the product label and full ingredient list (visible on the product page or on the product packaging).
  • Perform a patch test by applying a small amount of properly diluted product to a small area of the dog's skin and observing for 24 hours before full use.
  • Discontinue use immediately if any signs of irritation, redness, swelling, excessive scratching, or unusual behaviour occur.
  • Consult a licensed veterinarian if any adverse reaction occurs or if you have concerns about your dog's suitability for the product.

Furgenics is not liable for adverse reactions resulting from (i) use of products contrary to label instructions; (ii) use on dogs with pre-existing skin conditions not previously evaluated by a veterinarian; (iii) use in combination with other products not recommended for concurrent use; (iv) use after the product's expiry or stability date; (v) failure to perform a patch test prior to first use; or (vi) any other use that does not conform to the intended use described in Section 10.

If your dog experiences an adverse reaction to a Furgenics product, please contact info@furgenics.com. We will refund the product purchase price under our Refund Policy regardless of the cause of the reaction. This refund commitment is a customer-service practice and is not a representation that the product caused the reaction or an admission of liability.
