Why a hypoallergenic 2-in-1 exists as a category

A two-step sensitive-skin protocol (shampoo, rinse, conditioner, rinse) is the gold standard for sensitive-skin clients. For high-volume salons running 12+ baths a day, the time math doesn't always work — every extra rinse cycle compounds across the schedule. 2-in-1 chemistry is the practical response: enough conditioning built into the wash that a separate conditioner pass isn't required.

The trick is keeping the hypoallergenic profile intact. Most general-purpose 2-in-1 products lean heavy on fragrance or use surfactants that strip sensitive coats. Furgenics 2-in-1 Hypoallergenic is built on the same gentle base as the dedicated Furgenics Hypoallergenic Shampoo ([[PRICE:hypoallergenic-shampoo-gallon]]) — aloe and oat for soothing, tearless for safety around the eyes — but with the conditioning agents calibrated to deliver enough slip in a single pass.

The result: hypoallergenic performance equivalent to the two-step protocol on most sensitive-skin clients, at half the bath time. For clients with severe allergies or active skin reactions, the dedicated two-step is still the better choice.

How to dilute and use

  1. Brush out any surface debris before water. Important on short-coated breeds prone to dander accumulation in skin folds.
  2. Saturate with warm water until fully wet to the skin. Short coats wet quickly — 1–2 minutes is usually enough.
  3. Dilute at [[VALUE:dilution-ratio]] in a mixing bottle. For heavily soiled coats or active irritation, ready-to-use undiluted on problem areas is acceptable.
  4. Apply along the back and work into a rich lather over the whole coat. The 2-in-1 format means no separate conditioner pass — the conditioning is built into the wash.
  5. Massage 2–3 minutes to let the aloe and oat work on sensitive skin. For active irritation, extend to 4–5 minutes.
  6. Rinse fully until water runs clear. Tearless formulation means no special eye-protection technique required, but residual product on already-sensitive skin can still compound irritation.
  7. Towel-dry or HV dry on moderate setting — no separate conditioner step needed.

For severe sensitivities or chronic active reactions, escalate to the dedicated two-step protocol: Furgenics Hypoallergenic Shampoo ([[PRICE:hypoallergenic-shampoo-gallon]]) + Furgenics Oatmeal & Aloe Conditioner ([[PRICE:oatmeal-aloe-conditioner-gallon]]).
