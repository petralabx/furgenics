Why a rebuild conditioner is different from a maintenance conditioner

Most conditioners coat the surface of the hair shaft. Deep moisturizing conditioners need to actually penetrate damaged sections — the cuticle gaps in coats that have been sun-damaged, chemical-processed, or post-clip-shaved without recovery. The chemistry escalation between a maintenance conditioner and a rebuild conditioner is real.

Argan oil is the penetrating active. Its fatty acid profile is small enough to enter the cortex of damaged hair shafts rather than just sitting on the cuticle, where it restores lipid content lost to environmental wear. Shea butter handles the barrier layer — heavier than argan, it forms a lipid seal that holds moisture in the coat against the post-bath dry-out. Hydrolyzed oats add water-binding capacity: the protein-derived molecules attract and hold water during the leave-in step, which is why hydrolyzed oats specifically (not just oat flour) are the right inclusion for a deep-conditioning formula.

The result is a conditioner that does what a heavier coat actually needs — restore depth, not just smooth the surface.

How to dilute and use

This is the conditioner step in the intensive-conditioning protocol. Use after any Furgenics shampoo on damaged or high-maintenance coats.

  1. Confirm shampoo is fully rinsed before applying. Residual shampoo dilutes the conditioning chemistry and reduces penetration on damaged coats.
  2. Dilute at [[VALUE:dilution-ratio]] in a mixing bottle. For severely damaged or sun-bleached coats, ready-to-use undiluted on the worst sections is appropriate — this is what the rebuild capacity is for.
  3. Apply along the back and work through the entire coat, root to tip. Pay particular attention to the ends of long coats and any visibly damaged sections.
  4. Leave on 5–7 minutes — longer than a maintenance conditioner. Argan needs time to penetrate the cortex of damaged hair shafts; cutting this short loses most of the rebuild effect.
  5. Rinse fully until water runs clear. The heavier shea content can leave coat-weight if under-rinsed.
  6. Towel-dry and HV dry on moderate heat, working through with hands or a slicker. The shine effect builds during dry as the lipid seal sets.
