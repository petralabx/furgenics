Why real lavender essential oil matters for the spa experience

The lavender market has two tiers: products that use synthetic "lavender-type" fragrance compounds, and products built around actual lavender essential oil. The scent profile, the calming effect, and what stays on the coat after dry-out are different across those tiers.

Furgenics Lavender Spa uses real lavender essential oil rather than reverse-engineered fragrance chemistry. The aromatic compounds come from steam-distilled lavender flowers, which produces a genuine post-bath spa-grade scent that lingers on the coat for days rather than fading within hours.

The base is calibrated for general salon use — works on all coat types, rinses cleanly, doesn't leave residual product weight. Best results come on long, curly, and double coats where the coat structure holds the scent better; short-coated breeds get the experience but the scent fades faster as the coat has less surface area to carry the essential oil through dry-out.

Coat types: all coat types, especially medium-length, long, curly, and double coats where scent retention is best. Less effective at sustained scent on very short or smooth coats (the experience is still there during bath — it just fades faster post-dry).

How to dilute and use

  1. Brush out any visible mats or surface debris before water. The lavender essential oil binds to clean coat better than to debris-laden coat.
  2. Saturate with warm water until fully wet to the skin. The scent activates more strongly on warm, fully-wet coat.
  3. Dilute at [[VALUE:dilution-ratio]] in a mixing bottle. For premium spa service, ready-to-use undiluted is appropriate — the scent intensity reads as the upcharged service.
  4. Apply along the back and work down, massaging into a rich lather over the whole coat. Allow time for the aromatherapy effect; this is the part the dog actually notices.
  5. Massage 2–3 minutes for general grooming, or 4–5 minutes for the premium spa-service experience where the scent intensity is the deliverable.
  6. Rinse fully until water runs clear. The essential oil binds to the coat shaft during massage — over-rinsing won't remove the post-dry scent.
  7. Follow with a conditioner appropriate to the coat type. For the spa-service finish, Furgenics Deep Moisturizing Conditioner ([[PRICE:deep-moisturizing-conditioner-gallon]]) is the matching premium-tier pair.
