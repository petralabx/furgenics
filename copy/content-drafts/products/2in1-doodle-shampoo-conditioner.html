Why doodle coats need a different chemistry than straight or double coats

Doodle coats are structurally different from straight or double-coated breeds. The curl pattern traps dead hair and debris in the coat rather than shedding it out, so a routine wash leaves matted spots behind unless the formula has enough slip to release the tangles during the bath itself.

Argan oil and shea butter handle the conditioning — heavy enough to penetrate the coat shaft and add slip without weighing down the wave pattern. Coconut oil reinforces moisture retention as doodle coats lose hydration over the wash cycle. Silk amino acids fill gaps in damaged sections of the hair shaft, tuned for the kinkier doodle structure. Chamomile adds a mild anti-irritant for clients with sensitive skin under the coat.

The 2-in-1 format is the practical pick for production salons — one step, comparable slip to a separate shampoo-plus-conditioner pass on doodle coats, half the bath time on a tight booking day.

How to dilute and use

  1. Brush out the coat before water — important on doodles where matting hides under the wave. Use a slicker to break up visible mats before bath.
  2. Saturate the coat fully with warm water. Doodles wet slower than straight-coated breeds; expect 4–6 minutes on a fully-coated Bernedoodle.
  3. Dilute at [[VALUE:dilution-ratio]] in a mixing bottle. For severely matted coats, ready-to-use undiluted on the problem areas before rinsing through is acceptable.
  4. Apply along the back and work down, massaging through the curl pattern with hands or a curry comb. The 2-in-1 format means no separate conditioner pass — the slip is built into the wash.
  5. Massage 3–4 minutes to let the silk amino acids and conditioning oils penetrate the coat shaft.
  6. Rinse fully until water runs clear. Retained product weighs down the wave pattern and shows up as flat-looking coat after dry.
  7. Brush while damp for best detangling results, then HV dry.

For older doodles, mat-prone coats, or particularly dry texture, follow with Furgenics Deep Moisturizing Conditioner ([[PRICE:deep-moisturizing-conditioner-gallon]]) as a second pass.
