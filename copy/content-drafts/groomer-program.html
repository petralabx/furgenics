Canadian Groomer Program: free professional sample pack

How the Canadian program works

  1. Apply via the form at the bottom of this page. Tell us your salon name, location, and how many groomers are on bench.
  2. We verify within 1–2 business days. Light check on business legitimacy. Most salons clear in one day.

US Groomer Program: first-order gallon discount

Verified US grooming salons receive a first-order discount on a Furgenics professional gallon, applied at checkout (no separate code needed in most cases — the active discount campaign is shown in the banner below). Gallons ship via our US fulfillment partner, typically 3–5 business days domestic.

We're not running a US sample-pack program because our US fulfillment partner doesn't handle non-revenue shipments — cross-border sample shipping would defeat the speed benefit of having domestic US fulfillment in the first place. The first-order discount is structured to give US groomers the same effective trial economics: order a single gallon at the discounted price, work through a few weeks of bench time on it, and continue at standard pricing only if the formula holds up on your route.

Why the offers differ

Sample packs ship cheaply via Canada Post when fulfilled from our Vaughan, Ontario warehouse to anywhere inside Canada. Cross-border sample shipments would either take 7–14 days via international post or burn the unit economics of a free sample pack via expedited courier. Meanwhile, our US fulfillment is via a domestic US partner that handles paid shipments efficiently but doesn't accept non-revenue freight. Rather than offering US groomers a slower or worse version of the Canadian sample-pack program, we structured a first-order discount that gives the same outcome — a low-risk first gallon — through a different mechanism.

Frequently asked

Why do Canadian and US groomers get different offers?
Cross-border free-sample shipping is structurally expensive (either slow via international post or unprofitable via expedited courier), and our US fulfillment partner doesn't handle non-revenue shipments. Rather than offer US groomers a worse version of the sample-pack program, we structured the US offer as a first-order discount on a single gallon — same goal (low-risk trial), different mechanism.

How long do the 8oz samples last in a working salon?
Each 8oz sample, used at [[VALUE:dilution-ratio]] pro dilution, gives you roughly 4–6 dilutions of working wash for a typical mid-size dog. Eight samples cover a few weeks of normal testing across coat types.

Can I get a sample pack for each of my locations? (Canada)
The standard program ships one pack per verified business. Multi-location groups should reach out at info@furgenics.com — we run a separate flow for multi-location evaluation.

Can I get a multi-location discount? (US)
Yes — multi-location US groups should reach out at info@furgenics.com for a multi-location discount arrangement that covers the trial economics across all your sites.

What if my coat-type mix doesn't match the standard pack? (Canada)
We can swap one or two samples for alternative formulations on request. Note the request in the form's "anything else?" field.

How is this different from your retail customer first-order discount?
The retail first-order discount is a new-customer offer on any first gallon order — anyone can use it once. The Groomer Program is a verified-groomer benefit that's structured around your bench setup. In Canada, that means a free sample pack before any purchase. In the US, that means a discount on your first gallon that's typically more favorable than the retail new-customer discount once verification clears.

What's the catch?
There isn't one. Sample packs and first-order discounts both cost us a modest acquisition expense per groomer onboarded. We make it back when your salon switches to a gallon order; we lose a small marketing cost if you don't. That's an acceptable trade for getting our formula into a real working environment.
