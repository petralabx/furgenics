Furgenics is a professional dog grooming product line built for salon operators who care about chemistry as much as cleanliness. We make concentrated shampoos and conditioners — every gallon is 16:1 dilution, which means one gallon of our concentrate becomes 17 working gallons in your salon.

We're based in Vaughan, Ontario, with formulations developed and produced in Canada. Every product is dog-specific (no cat formulas, no human-product crossovers), pH-balanced for canine skin, and tested at the salon level before we ship it.

Why we exist

The professional dog grooming product market splits into two ends: ultra-premium imports at $40–60 per gallon equivalent, and generic-value products that trade quality for price. The middle of the market — high-quality concentrated formulas at honest prices — is where most working salons actually live, and it's underserved.

Furgenics is built for that middle. $24.99 CAD per gallon, 16:1 concentrate, full INCI transparency, and no marketing fluff.

What we make

A focused line of professional shampoos and conditioners for common salon needs: hypoallergenic and gentle formulations, oatmeal and aloe for sensitive skin, 2-in-1 formulas for doodles and curly coats, deshedding for double-coats, lavender spa for calming finishes, deep moisturizing for damaged coats, and conditioners to pair with our shampoos.

Sample sizes are available free through our Groomer Program — eight bottles, no purchase obligation, so you can test on your bench before committing to gallons.
