## Dilution and use

### How do I dilute Furgenics shampoo 16:1?
Mix 1 part Furgenics shampoo with 16 parts warm water in a mixing bottle. One 128oz (1 gallon) bottle at 16:1 produces up to 17 working gallons. This works out to roughly the cost of a cup of coffee per working gallon at pro dilution, making it ideal for high-volume grooming salons.

### Can I use Furgenics ready-to-use without diluting?
Yes. For heavier soiling or when a richer lather is preferred, Furgenics shampoos can be used ready-to-use straight from the bottle. For most grooming scenarios, dilution up to 16:1 gives effective cleaning with significant per-wash cost savings. Professional salons typically dilute for volume work.

### How long should I leave the shampoo on the coat?
For most Furgenics formulas, lather the shampoo through the coat and allow it to sit for 2–5 minutes before rinsing. Oatmeal and aloe formulas benefit from the longer end (3–5 minutes) for maximum soothing on dry or irritated skin. Always rinse thoroughly.

### Can I use Furgenics shampoo on puppies?

## Product selection

### Which Furgenics shampoo is best for Goldendoodles?
For Goldendoodles, we recommend the 2-in-1 Doodle Shampoo & Conditioner Gallon (FUR-020). It's purpose-built for curly, mat-prone coats with silk amino acids, argan oil, coconut oil, shea butter, and chamomile. For very dry or damaged doodle coats, follow up with Deep Moisturizing Conditioner (FUR-021) for extra hydration.

### Which Furgenics shampoo is best for Huskies and Shepherds?
For heavy-shedding double-coated breeds like Huskies, German Shepherds, and Golden Retrievers, we recommend the Deshedding Shampoo Gallon (FUR-013). It loosens undercoat during bathing with hydrolyzed keratin and safflower oil, making post-bath brushing and de-shedding dramatically more effective.

### Which Furgenics shampoo is best for sensitive skin?
For dogs with allergies, sensitive skin, or irritation, we recommend the Hypoallergenic Dog Shampoo Gallon (FUR-001). It's tearless, sulfate-free, and paraben-free, safe for regular professional use on breeds prone to skin sensitivities like French Bulldogs, Boxers, and Pitbulls. For a time-saving one-step version, try the 2-in-1 Hypoallergenic (FUR-005).

### What's the difference between a shampoo and a 2-in-1?
A dedicated shampoo cleanses; a conditioner (applied after) moisturizes and detangles. A 2-in-1 combines both steps into one application, saving time in high-volume salons. Furgenics offers both: dedicated shampoos like FUR-001 and matching conditioners like FUR-010, plus 2-in-1 options like FUR-005 (hypoallergenic) and FUR-020 (doodle-specific).
