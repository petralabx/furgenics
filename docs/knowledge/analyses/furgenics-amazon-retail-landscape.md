## Why this analysis exists

Furgenics' primary channel is direct-to-salon via furgenics.com. Amazon is a planned secondary channel per the January 2026 GTM workbook. Before that launch goes live, this synthesis captures: who's already in that market, what they charge, how they position, and where Furgenics has product-line gaps and pricing decisions to make.

This compounds forward. When the AEO tracker starts measuring citation rate for prompts like "best professional dog shampoo gallon on Amazon", we can compare observed citations against this baseline map of who actually sells there.

## The Amazon market in one picture

Seven tracked brands in the Jan 2026 competitive set. Ranked by estimated monthly revenue across their tracked SKUs:

1. **Warren London** — ~$413k/mo total (**but 93% of it is one outlier SKU**). Without Hydrating Butter Leave-In: ~$27k.
2. **Bark2Basics** — ~$138k/mo. Broadest line. Oatmeal Shampoo is the tracked-category leader.

7. **We Love Doodles** — ~$5k/mo. Niche, doodle-specific, half-gallon format.

Estimates come from the workbook's embedded figures (likely Jungle Scout or similar Amazon data tool). Treat them as directional, not audited.

## Three structural observations

### 1. Warren London's Leave-In Conditioner is the outlier worth investigating

$385,915/mo estimated on a single SKU (B0C7HKYR92, 6,433 monthly units, 4.5 stars across 16,223 reviews). That's more revenue than every other tracked SKU in the competitive set combined. Two possibilities:

- **A genuine breakout product.** Possible — 16,223 reviews doesn't materialize from nothing. If real, this signals substantial demand for a leave-in conditioner product category Furgenics doesn't currently serve.
- **A data estimation inflation.** Third-party Amazon estimation tools can produce outliers when BSR signals spike briefly. Worth verifying via Keepa or a second estimation source before treating as ground truth.

Either way, the product category itself — leave-in conditioner — is a gap in the Furgenics line that appears in the TBD roster (`FUR-006?` in the January 2026 workbook) but is not yet launched. This is probably the single highest-value missing SKU to ship.

### 2. The "concentrate war" has two camps and Furgenics is closer to one

- **High-concentration camp (16:1 to 32:1):** Coat Handler (up to 32:1), Bark2Basics (16:1-32:1). Positioning: professional, dilution economics, Amazon sales leadership in deshedding + oatmeal.
- **Low-concentration camp (ready-to-use or mild dilution):** Warren London (no clear dilution story), We Love Doodles (no dilution), most of Bio-Groom (2:1 to 4:1).
- **Middle (10:1-15:1):** PetAg, Bubble Bros (12:1), some Bio-Groom SKUs (12:1).

Furgenics is 16:1 across the line. That puts it in the high-concentration camp with Coat Handler and Bark2Basics — which are also the Amazon volume leaders in the categories they contest. Structurally, the dilution-math positioning is defensible, and matches where the professional-volume revenue actually sits.

### 3. The pricing band gap

Amazon market prices (USD, per gallon):

| Range | Where | Brands |
|---|---|---|
| $29.99 | Half-gallon niche | We Love Doodles |
| $35.99–$39.99 | Value tier | Bubble Bros, Warren London (non-leave-in) |
| $44.99–$49.99 | Middle tier (most volume) | Bio-Groom base line, PetAg, Bark2Basics Oatmeal, Coat Handler Hypoallergenic |
| $50–$64 | Premium tier | Coat Handler Deshedding, Bark2Basics Hypoallergenic, Bio-Groom 2-in-1 |
| $89 | Ultra-premium | Bio-Groom Anti-Shed Deshedding Conditioner |

Furgenics direct-to-salon price is **$19 USD / gallon**. That's **53% below the cheapest tracked Amazon competitor and ~60% below the median**.

This is a deliberate DTC-positioning choice, not an error, and works fine for direct-to-salon relationships where the brand owns the experience. On Amazon it's a different question. Extreme-underpricing in a category can read as "cheap" rather than "generous" — professional buyers on Amazon have been trained to distrust prices sharply below the middle tier. Plausible Amazon pricing strategies to evaluate before launch:

- **Match the middle tier ($44.99).** Cleanest signal. Preserves margin. Ignores the DTC-loyalty risk entirely.
- **Sit just below the middle ($39.99).** Value challenger. Competes with Bubble Bros on price while matching Bio-Groom/PetAg on perceived quality tier. Risks creating Amazon-vs-direct price sensitivity with existing salon accounts.
- **Match DTC ($19 USD).** Maximum disruption. High volume risk, thin margin on a channel with ~15-30% Amazon fees. Also creates channel conflict with direct-to-salon customers who pay more.
- **Dynamic by SKU.** Premium-priced on Hypoallergenic (where Bio-Groom sets a high anchor) + match middle on commodity SKUs (Oatmeal).

This is a Stephen decision, not an algorithmic one. Flagged.

## What Furgenics does NOT compete on (and arguably shouldn't)

- **Ultra-premium single-SKU ($89+).** Bio-Groom owns that tier with one product. Low volume, high margin, brand-heritage-priced. Not a Furgenics strategy.
- **Half-gallon formats.** We Love Doodles' market is small ($5k/mo total) and format-fragmented. Furgenics' concentrate economics work against half-gallon packaging anyway (lower working-gallon yield per ounce purchased).
- **Consumer-retail branding (Tropiclean-style).** Mixing pro and retail positioning confuses groomers and dilutes brand trust.

## Implications for the AEO tracker (v1)

The current 12 tracked prompts in `target-queries.md` should be reviewed against this competitive landscape. Specifically:

1. **Add at least one leave-in-conditioner query.** It's currently zero-weighted in our AEO tracking even though the Amazon data suggests it may be the single largest category. If Furgenics launches FUR-006 (Leave-In), we want the baseline measurement to exist.
2. **Verify "deshedding shampoo" prompts weight toward Coat Handler.** They're the category leader on Amazon and likely in AI engine citations too. If our tracker isn't seeing them, tracker logic may be missing them.
3. **Bark2Basics is probably under-tracked.** Largest Amazon volume, not in the current `competitor-intel.md` pre-2026-04-22 seven-competitor set. Monitor specifically for "best oatmeal dog shampoo" and "bulk dog grooming shampoo" cases.
4. **Warren London is an AEO dark horse.** Their review count (16,223) suggests brand recognition strong enough to surface in general-purpose AI responses. Watch for them in unexpected places.

## Actionable decisions surfaced

Not recommendations, but decisions this data reveals are open:

1. **Launch Leave-In Conditioner (FUR-006)?** Product category appears to be the largest Amazon SKU by revenue. Category is absent from Furgenics' line.
2. **Amazon pricing strategy.** Default to DTC pricing ($19 USD) would be channel-conflicting and margin-destructive. Alternative tiers need explicit choice.
3. **Deshedding concentrate ratio.** Coat Handler leads the category at 32:1 while Furgenics is 16:1. Decision: match or differentiate.
4. **Ingredient list gap.** FUR-001 and FUR-011 still missing INCI lists as of Jan 2026 workbook. Can't launch on Amazon without these (they're regulatory-required).
5. **SKU reconciliation.** Jan 2026 workbook uses `FUR-026` + `FUR-037` for 2-in-1 Hypoallergenic + 2-in-1 Doodle; Shopify uses `FUR-005` + `FUR-020`. Confirm canonical.
