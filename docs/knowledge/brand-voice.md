# Furgenics — Brand Voice

> Canonical tone and style guide. Any auto-generated content (blog posts, page copy, AEO answer blocks, FAQ answers) must conform to this voice.

## Audience

**Primary:** Professional dog groomers (salon owners, mobile groomers, shop employees)
**Secondary:** Boarding facility operators, veterinary clinic managers, breeders
**NOT the audience:** Pet owners shopping retail (they'll encounter our content indirectly via search, but we write for professionals)

## Voice attributes

| Attribute | Dial |
|---|---|
| Formal ↔ Casual | **Slight casual** — confident professional tone, not corporate |
| Technical ↔ Accessible | **Technical** — groomers know dilution ratios, coat types, surfactants. Speak to them as peers. |
| Warm ↔ Clinical | **Warm** — we care about groomers' businesses, not just sales |
| Concise ↔ Thorough | **Thorough but ruthless** — every sentence earns its place |
| Sales-y ↔ Educational | **90% educational, 10% sales** — the product sells itself through detailed product knowledge |

## Tone rules

- **Lead with the professional use case, not the dog.** "For salons running high volume" beats "Your dog will love this."
- **Assume expertise.** Groomers know what "double coat" means. Don't over-explain. Do explain WHY a formula works for that coat type.
- **Respect the economics.** Groomers run businesses. Per-working-gallon cost, time savings, client retention — these matter more than cute copy.
- **Use specific breeds, not generalities.** "Good for double-coated breeds like Huskies, German Shepherds, Goldens" beats "Good for shedding dogs."
- **Cite the formula specifics.** "Colloidal oatmeal and aloe vera calm irritation" beats "soothing ingredients."
- **Own the pricing clarity.** "$24.99 CAD per gallon, dilutes 16:1 for up to 17 working gallons, so roughly the cost of a cup of coffee per working gallon at pro dilution."

## Forbidden phrases and topics

### Brand privacy
- ❌ **NEVER mention "Petra Lab-X"** in any public-facing Furgenics content (blog, PDP, page, FAQ, schema, meta description, alt text)
- ❌ **NEVER reveal contract manufacturing relationship** in public copy
- ✅ Use "Made in Canada" — that's the acceptable origin statement

### Medical / treatment claims
- ❌ Do NOT say shampoo "treats," "cures," or "heals" skin conditions
- ❌ Do NOT claim veterinary efficacy without explicit vet endorsement
- ✅ Use "soothes," "calms," "supports," "helps relieve"

### Pet-owner retail vocabulary (we're B2B)
- ❌ "Your furry friend"
- ❌ "Pampered pup"
- ❌ "Pet parents"
- ❌ "Give your dog a treat"
- ✅ "Professional groomers," "salon clients," "the dog," "coats," "bath time"

### Generic marketing filler
- ❌ "In today's fast-paced world"
- ❌ "Look no further"
- ❌ "Unlock the power of"
- ❌ "Revolutionize your grooming"
- ❌ Exclamation marks in body copy (reserve for CTAs)

### Platform violations
- ❌ No medical drug comparisons (cannot say "like Benadryl" etc.)
- ❌ No direct competitor disparagement (can mention competitor names in neutral comparison contexts; never say "better than X")

## Signature phrasings (use these freely — they're brand-owned)

- "One 128oz gallon makes up to 17 working gallons"
- "Roughly the cost of a cup of coffee per working gallon at pro dilution"
- "Built for professional salons"
- "Rich lather, fast rinse, consistent results"
- "Free of sulfates, parabens, and synthetic dyes" (only for shampoos that actually are)
- "Test the formula on your clients at half the price"
- "New to Furgenics?" (intro to FUR50 discount callout)

## Structural patterns

### PDP descriptions follow this structure:
1. **Opening paragraph (40-60 words)** — answer-first: what is this product, who is it for, key benefit, dilution economics
2. **Second paragraph** — deeper context on formulation and use case
3. **"Key features" list** — 5 bulleted features with short explanations
4. **"Who this shampoo is for" list** — target customer segments
5. **"Best for these coat types and breeds"** — explicit coat types and breeds
6. **"How to dilute and use"** — numbered step-by-step
7. **"Pairs well with"** — 2-3 internal links to related products
8. **"First order? Save 50%"** — FUR50 discount callout with standard messaging

### Blog post structure:
1. **H1 title** — include primary query keyword (e.g., "Best shampoo for Goldendoodles")
2. **Answer-block (40-60 words)** — direct answer to the post title as opening paragraph
3. **"Why this matters for professional groomers"** — business angle
4. **Deep content sections** — H2/H3 structure mirroring related AEO prompts
5. **"Products that fit this use case"** — internal links to relevant gallons
6. **FAQ section** — 3-5 questions with 40-60 word answers (feeds FAQ schema)
7. **Closing CTA** — groomer program signup or FUR50 discount

### FAQ answer structure:
- **40-60 word answers** — optimal for both featured snippets and AEO citation
- **Lead with the direct answer**, then 1-2 supporting sentences
- **Include one specific fact** (a number, a breed, a condition) per answer
- **Mention the product by name** when relevant

## Accessibility in copy

- Don't rely on color alone (PDPs are color-styled, copy must still make sense in grayscale)
- Alt text is content, not decoration — describe what's in the image with SEO keywords naturally included
- Headings must be hierarchical (H1 → H2 → H3, no skips)
